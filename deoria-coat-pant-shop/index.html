Getting the fit right

A cheap suit that fits beats an expensive suit that does not, and it is not close. Four checks we run on every customer before they leave:

  1. Shoulder seam. It should end where your shoulder ends. This is the one thing that cannot be altered cheaply — everything else can.
  2. Sleeve length. About half an inch of shirt cuff showing. More looks borrowed, less looks outgrown.
  3. Button stance. With the top button closed there should be no X-shaped pull across your chest.
  4. Trouser break. A single soft fold over the shoe. Bring the shoes you will actually wear.

Buy or rent?

We do both, and the honest answer depends on how often you will wear it. Rent the tuxedo or the heavily embroidered wedding suit you will use once. Buy the navy or charcoal two-piece — you will reach for it again within the year, and a suit that has been altered to your body will always sit better than one that has been altered to somebody else's.

Frequently asked questions

Do you keep coat pant in stock or is everything stitched to order?

Both. Most sizes are on the rack so you can try the piece on and see the drape before deciding. We then alter sleeve length, trouser length and waist to your measurement. If you want a specific fabric or colour that is not on the floor, tell us your date and we will arrange it.

How long does alteration take?

Simple length and waist adjustments are usually same-day or next-day. Shoulder and full-body reshaping needs longer, so come in at least a week before your function if the fit needs real work.

Can I rent a coat pant instead of buying it?

Yes. We offer both purchase and rental. Rental suits most people who need a tuxedo or heavy wedding suit for one function; buying makes more sense for a navy or charcoal suit you will reuse for office, interviews and other people's weddings.

What should I bring for the fitting?

Wear or carry the shoes you plan to use, since trouser length depends on heel height. If you have a shirt you intend to pair with the suit, bring that too.
