Pitching the outfit at the right level

The most common engagement mistake is overdressing. Grooms arrive at the ring ceremony in something close to full wedding regalia, and two things follow: they are uncomfortable in a room that did not call for it, and the wedding photographs a few months later have nothing left to escalate to.

Think of it as a deliberate step down. If the wedding sherwani is a ten, the engagement look should be a seven — sharp, considered, and clearly an occasion outfit, but not the headline.

Colours for a ring ceremony

  • Daytime, at home: pastels — powder blue, mint, sand, off-white. Light fabrics, minimal work.
  • Evening, in a hall: navy, charcoal, bottle green, wine. Deeper shades hold up under artificial light.
  • Avoid: pure black if the ceremony is traditional and elders are present — it still reads as sombre to some families. Midnight blue gives you the same effect without the concern.

The ring-hand detail nobody plans for

Every engagement produces the same photograph: a close-up of two hands and a ring. Your sleeve is in that frame, at that exact distance, and it is one of the few shots that will be printed and framed. Two practical consequences — make sure the jacket sleeve and shirt cuff are properly finished with about half an inch of cuff showing, and consider a cufflink or a clean button placket rather than whatever the shirt came with. It is a small thing that shows up large.

Getting the fit sorted in time

Engagement outfits need less lead time than wedding wear, but they still need a fitting. Come in two to three weeks ahead, wearing or carrying the shoes you plan to use. We will alter sleeve, waist and trouser length at the showroom, and if anything needs a second look there is still room in the calendar for it.

Frequently asked questions

What should a man wear to his own engagement?

Something one level below your wedding outfit in weight and detail. A well-fitted three-piece suit, a bandhgala, or a clean indo-western set all work. Save the heavy embroidery for the wedding — if the engagement look is as elaborate as the shaadi look, the wedding photographs lose their impact.

Suit or ethnic for a ring ceremony?

Both are correct; it depends on the setting. A hall or hotel function leans western — a three-piece or bandhgala. A ceremony at home with elders present leans ethnic — an indo-western set or a light kurta with a jacket. If you are unsure, a bandhgala is the safe middle: formal enough for a hall, Indian enough for the family.

Should my outfit match my fiancée's?

Coordinate, do not match. Ask what colour she is wearing and pick something in the same tonal family at a different intensity. Identical shades photograph as a costume; a considered contrast photographs as a couple. Bring a photo and we will hold fabrics against it.

Can I rent an engagement outfit?

Yes. Rental works well here, though many men choose to buy a three-piece for an engagement specifically because it is one wedding outfit they will genuinely reuse — for other people's weddings, for work, for interviews.

How early should I book?

Two to three weeks is usually enough for an engagement, since the outfits are less specialised than wedding wear. Allow four weeks if the ceremony falls in peak wedding season, when alteration queues are longer.
